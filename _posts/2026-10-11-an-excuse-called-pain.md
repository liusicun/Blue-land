In my previous post, I spent almost the entire time talking about my experience of pain. I tried different ways to explain it, tried to find a definitive answer or, to be more accurate, the excuse for it. And tried to justify why I was feeling that way. But funny thing is, after describing myself as the most miserable person in the world, I started asking myself, am I really suffering that bad? Because, I have absolutely no idea what I was suffering from. I just feel like I’m in pain, and that’s all. So if I can’t even explain what exactly is going on with me, does it really exist?

Or maybe I’m just using pain as an excuse to justify all the shitty things going on in my life. So that I can simply brainwash myself that I’m suffering so much, so that I can’t get things done correctly or perfectly. But it does sound like a good excuse. xD

Although I really want to know, I just don’t. Maybe I’m not actually suffering as much as I think I am. Maybe I’m just a fucking cunning person who has figured out how to use his own pain as an excuse for everything.

Somehow, I’d like to think that I’m a nice person, at least not a bad guy. When it comes to how I treat others, as long as I haven’t noticed any deliberate malice from others, I have always tried to treat them with sincerity.

I value sincerity a lot. Of course, I do understand that human nature is quite complicated. Some people might not even realize that there’s something wrong with the way they treat others and sure sometimes I do feel upset about how other people treated me but even then I have never really wanted to hurt anyone. I have my own boundaries. When someone crosses them, I usually just cut them off, and then start regretting the fact that I was stupid enough to misjudge another person again. But I have never wanted a revenge. Not even once.

I once met someone who was genuinely kind, his kindness was like the warm sun. I don’t really know how else to describe it. He was just that kind of person. And I wanted to become someone like him. But later on I realized how incredibly difficult that actually is, and it also makes me feel like I should at least try even harder to become that kind of person, and then… you know the story.
